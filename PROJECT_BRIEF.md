# GurunanakStore — Project Brief

## Store and customers

- **Store name:** GurunanakStore
- **Products:** Motorcycle and bike accessories.
- **Customers:** All kinds of riders.
- **Brand feel:** Premium, practical, and trustworthy.

## Launch range

- **Launch size:** About 1–10 products.
- **Typical price:** Around ₹500–₹2,000.
- **Product photos:** The owner has real photos. Temporary local illustrations are used until those files are added to the project.
- **Product details:** Exact stock, product names, specifications, and final prices still need to be supplied.

## Pages and shopping

- Home, Shop, Product detail, About, Contact, Cart, and Checkout.
- The cart should remember items in the browser.
- Customers should be able to place an order through a pre-filled WhatsApp message.
- Customers should be able to choose UPI. A UPI ID and QR code must be supplied before real UPI payments can be accepted.
- Online checkout/payment account details have not yet been supplied.

## Design direction

- **Colours:** A premium black-and-gold direction with clean navy and silver accents.
- **Inspiration:** Amazon or Flipkart for the shopping experience.
- **Fonts:** Strong display lettering with clear, easy-to-read body text.
- **Domain:** Not decided yet.
